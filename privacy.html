Privacy notice

This website sets no cookies of its own and has no tracking. This notice explains what I do collect when you use the booking form, email me or hire me.

Last updated 27 October 2026

The short version
  • This website sets no cookies of its own and has no tracking or analytics.
  • The booking form collects only what you type into it, and sends it to me. It's stored in Google Sheets.
  • The booking page has a Cloudflare bot check, so spam can't fill up my bookings.
  • If you email me or hire me, I keep what's needed to do the job.
  • Back-ups go on my encrypted SSD and are deleted 14 days after I hand your Mac back.
  • I don't sell your data or send marketing.

Who I am

Neobitium is a trading name of Ben Mulliss, a sole trader. I decide how your personal data is used, which makes me the data controller.

Email: neobitium@outlook.com

What this website collects

This website doesn't set cookies of its own, and it doesn't use analytics, tracking pixels or adverts. It stores nothing of its own in your browser. The one thing it loads from another company is the Cloudflare bot check on the booking page, described below. See the cookie notice.

The package builder works out a price in your browser and sends nothing. The only thing the website collects is what you type into the booking form and choose to send:

  • your name and email address, and your phone number if you give one
  • the kind of Mac you have, its model and year, and the macOS it has now
  • the services you chose and the price shown
  • whether you'll bring or post your Mac
  • anything you write in the message box
  • that you ticked the confirmations, and the version of the terms you saw

When you send the form, it goes to my booking system. This is a small Google Apps Script that adds your request to a Google Sheets spreadsheet, both provided by Google. Pressing the send button opens a confirmation page from Google in a new tab. That page is run by Google, not by this website. Please don't put passwords or bank details in the form.

The bot check (Cloudflare Turnstile)

The booking form includes a check from Cloudflare, called Turnstile, that works out whether you're a real person or an automated bot. I use it to stop spam. The check only loads on the booking page.

  • When the page loads, your browser contacts Cloudflare. Cloudflare says Turnstile processes your IP address, your browser's TLS fingerprint and User-Agent (the name and version of your browser), and the address of this website.
  • When you send the form, the result of the check goes to my booking system, which asks Cloudflare to confirm it. My booking spreadsheet doesn't record your IP address.
  • Cloudflare says it uses this only to detect and block bots, and not to identify or profile people. For this, Cloudflare acts as my processor. Cloudflare also uses the same signals to improve Turnstile, and for that it is a controller in its own right. Read the Cloudflare Turnstile privacy addendum (opens in a new tab) for details.
  • If you can't use the check, email me instead and I'll deal with your request by email.

The booking page also includes a hidden field that only a robot would fill in. If it's filled in, I throw the request away.

Like any website, the server that hosts this one (GoDaddy) may keep technical logs, such as your IP address and the pages requested, to keep the site secure and running. I don't use these logs to identify you.

What I collect when you email me or hire me

  • Emails and booking requests: your name, email address, details of your Mac and your message.
  • Jobs: your contact details, your postal address if I'm posting your Mac back, details of your Mac, what we agreed, what I did, and a record of payment. If you pay by bank transfer, I also see the details your bank shows with the payment.
  • Back-ups: if you choose a back-up, I copy your whole Mac with Time Machine, which may include personal data such as photos, documents and messages. I copy it to make the back-up. I don't browse or read it, beyond checking that the copy is complete.
  • Passwords: to do the work, I need the password of an administrator account on your Mac, or of a new administrator account you create for me. I only use it for your job and don't keep it afterwards. Please change the password, or delete the account, once you have your Mac back. Please don't email passwords to me.

Why I use your data, and the legal reason

  • To reply to your email or booking request and confirm a job. This is taking steps at your request before a contract (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(b)).
  • To check that a booking request comes from a real person and not a bot, and to keep spam out of my bookings. This is my legitimate interest in keeping my booking system working (Article 6(1)(f)).
  • To do the job you have agreed. This is performing a contract (Article 6(1)(b)).
  • To keep accounts and tax records. This is a legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c)).
  • To deal with complaints and aftercare. This is my legitimate interest in sorting out problems fairly (Article 6(1)(f)).

I don't sell your data. I don't use it for marketing, and I won't send you marketing emails unless you ask me to.

Who else sees your data

  • My email provider, Outlook (Microsoft), which handles the emails we exchange.
  • My hosting provider, GoDaddy, for the technical logs described above.
  • Google, which provides the Google Sheets spreadsheet and the Google Apps Script that hold and process what you send through the booking form.
  • Cloudflare, which runs the bot check on the booking page.
  • HMRC, if I need to share records for tax.
  • My bank, if you pay by bank transfer.
  • A delivery company, if your Mac is posted to me or back to you.

Google and Cloudflare are international companies, and they may process data outside the UK. Where they do, they should use safeguards approved under UK law.

How long I keep your data

  • Emails and booking requests that don't lead to a job: deleted within 6 months. That includes the row in my booking spreadsheet.
  • Job records and payment records: kept for as long as tax law requires, and to deal with aftercare or complaints.
  • Back-ups: kept on my encrypted SSD for 14 days after I hand your Mac back, then deleted. I'll delete one sooner if you ask.

How I keep it safe

I keep back-ups on my own encrypted SSD, and I only use your files for the job you asked for. I delete them when the time above is up.

The booking spreadsheet is in my own Google account, and the booking form can only add new rows to it. It can't read anything back out.

Your rights

You can ask me to:

  • tell you what data I hold about you, and give you a copy
  • correct data that is wrong
  • delete your data
  • restrict how I use your data, or stop using it
  • give you your data in a portable format

Email me to use any of these. I'll reply within one month. If you aren't happy with how I've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint (opens in a new tab).

Photos on the portfolio page

The portfolio page shows screenshots from Macs I've upgraded, with their owners' agreement. The About This Mac windows show the Macs' serial numbers. A row of one owner's viewing history has been pixelated.

Children

My services, and the booking form, are for people aged 18 or over. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to get in touch. I don't knowingly collect data from children.
