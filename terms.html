Terms and conditions

The rules for hiring Neobitium, in plain English.

Last updated 27 October 2026

The short version
  • You book and pay up front. If I can't do the job, I refund you in full, not including postage costs.
  • You have 14 days to cancel after you book, as long as I haven't started the upgrade. Once I've started, there are no refunds.
  • I recommend a back-up. Without one, please don't rely on me for the only copy of anything important.
  • Every job comes with your legal rights, whether or not you buy aftercare.
  • Apple doesn't endorse this method, so some features may not work.

1. About these terms

These terms apply when you hire Neobitium, a trading name of Ben Mulliss, to work on your Mac. They apply once you've booked a job. Sending the booking form is a request, not a booking: a booking is made when I confirm it by email. When you send the form, you confirm that you've read these terms and the privacy notice.

If my confirmation email says something different for a particular job, my email applies to that job.

2. Who can hire me

You must be 18 or over, and you must own the Mac or have the owner's permission. If you are under 18, a parent or guardian must place the order.

3. Services and prices

  • My services are on the services page, and my prices are on the pricing page.
  • When you book, I confirm by email what's included, the price and your right to cancel. I don't start until you've replied.
  • The Mac software upgrade includes a basic test. The Peace-of-mind pack adds a full test and aftercare.
  • I only change the price of a job if you agree.
  • Prices are in pounds sterling.

4. Sending your Mac

  • When you book, I send you my address. You can post your Mac to me or bring it.
  • Please don't send or bring anything before you've booked.
  • Pack it carefully, and use a tracked, insured service if you post it.
  • In my booking email, I'll tell you how it will come back to you and what that costs, before you send anything.
  • When I receive your Mac, I note its condition and what you've asked me to do. I look after it while I have it, then send or hand it back with a short summary.

5. Back-ups and your data

  • I strongly recommend a back-up. Before I change anything, I make a full copy of your Mac with Time Machine onto my encrypted SSD, and check it's complete.
  • If something goes wrong, I can put your files back. A back-up is included in Go Full Neobitium and available as an add-on.
  • If Time Machine won't work on your Mac, I'll tell you and copy your files across by hand in Finder instead. That protects your files, but isn't a full copy of the whole Mac.
  • No software upgrade can be guaranteed to go perfectly, so please don't rely on me for the only copy of anything important.
  • I keep the back-up for 14 days after I hand your Mac back, then delete it.
  • If you decline a back-up, I'll confirm that by email before I start.

6. Upgrades Apple doesn't endorse

Apple doesn't endorse running newer macOS on older Macs. OpenCore Legacy Patcher is an independent open-source project. Some features may not work on an unsupported Mac. Features that need an Apple silicon chip or a T2 security chip won't work, and iPhone Mirroring is one example. Some macOS updates may need extra steps afterwards. I only use this method on Apple-branded Macs.

Before you book, I'll tell you what I expect to work and what might not. After the job, I'll tell you what I found.

7. If the upgrade can't be done

If I find during a job that your Mac can't run what we agreed, I'll stop and tell you. I'll put the Mac back as close as I can to how it was, and I'll refund you in full, not including postage costs.

8. Aftercare and your legal rights

  • Whether or not you buy aftercare, you keep your legal rights. If I don't do the work with reasonable care and skill, I'll put it right.
  • The Peace-of-mind pack and Go Full Neobitium add 14 days of aftercare. For 14 days after I give your Mac back, if something I installed or changed stops working as described in my summary, tell me and I'll fix it at no extra charge.
  • Aftercare doesn't cover physical damage, hardware that fails for a reason unrelated to my work, or changes you make after handover, including installing later macOS updates.

9. Your responsibilities

  • Give me the password of an administrator account on your Mac, or create a new administrator account for me and give me its password. Please don't email passwords.
  • Tell me about any firmware locks or Apple Activation Lock that I will need to deal with.
  • Give me accurate information about your Mac and what you want from it.
  • Pay as set out below.

10. Payment

Payment is due when you book. I accept cash or bank transfer, and I'll tell you how to pay when I confirm your booking. If you cancel in time, or I can't do the job, I'll refund you as set out in the refunds and cancellation policy.

11. Cancelling and refunds

You can cancel within 14 days of booking, as long as I haven't started the upgrade. Once I've started the upgrade, I can't stop it and there are no refunds, full or partial. See the refunds and cancellation policy.

12. If something goes wrong

I'll do the work with reasonable care and skill. If I don't, you have legal rights, including the right to ask me to repeat or fix the work, or to a price reduction.

I'm responsible for losses that are a foreseeable result of me breaking these terms or failing to use reasonable care. Nothing in these terms limits my liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, for fraud, or for anything else the law doesn't allow me to limit.

I'm not responsible for losses that weren't foreseeable. If you chose not to have a back-up after I warned you, I'm not responsible for data lost despite my reasonable care.

13. Complaints

If you're unhappy, email me at neobitium@outlook.com and I'll do my best to put it right. If we can't agree, I'll tell you in writing whether I'm willing to use an alternative dispute resolution (ADR) provider and, if so, which one. You can also get free advice from Citizens Advice: citizensadvice.org.uk/consumer (opens in a new tab).

14. Your data

How I use your personal data is explained in the privacy notice. That includes how booking requests are stored and how the bot check on the booking form works.

15. Independence and trademarks

Neobitium is independent. It isn't affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Apple Inc. or the OpenCore Legacy Patcher project. Product names belong to their owners.

16. Which law applies

These terms are governed by the law of England and Wales. If you live in Scotland or Northern Ireland, you can also bring a claim in the courts where you live.

17. Changes to these terms

If I change these terms, the new version applies to jobs agreed after the date at the top of the page.
