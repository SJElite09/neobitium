Refunds and cancellation

You can change your mind within 14 days. Here is how that works.

Last updated 27 October 2026

The short version
  • You can cancel within 14 days of booking, for any reason, as long as I haven't started the upgrade.
  • If I haven't started, you get a full refund, not including postage costs.
  • Once I've started the upgrade, there are no refunds, not even partial ones. I have to finish it, or your Mac won't work.
  • If I can't do the job, I refund you in full, not including postage costs.

Your 14-day right to cancel

If you book a job with me by email, through this website, or anywhere other than my own premises, you have the right to cancel within 14 days. You don't need to give a reason.

The 14 days start the day after you book.

How to cancel

Email neobitium@outlook.com and tell me you want to cancel. Use the template below if you wish.

If you ask me to start within the 14 days

I won't start work within the 14 days unless you ask me to by email. When you ask, I'll also ask you to confirm that you understand that once I've started the upgrade, you can't cancel and there are no refunds, not even partial ones.

Starting the upgrade means I've begun installing the new macOS on your Mac. Once I've begun, I have to finish it, because your Mac won't work properly until the upgrade is done. That's why I can't refund part of it.

Refunds

If you cancel in time and I haven't started the upgrade, I'll refund everything you've paid. Refunds don't include postage costs. If I've started the upgrade, there's no refund, full or partial. I'll refund you within 14 days of getting your cancellation. I'll use the same payment method you used, unless you agree otherwise. If you paid in cash, I'll agree with you how to refund it.

If the work isn't good enough

The 14-day right above is for changing your mind. Separately, if my work isn't done with reasonable care and skill, or doesn't match what we agreed, you have legal rights to have it repeated or fixed at no cost. If that can't be done in a reasonable time, you can ask for a price reduction, up to the full price. These rights don't affect your other legal rights.

If I can't do the job

If I find that your Mac can't run what we agreed, I'll refund you in full, not including postage costs. See section 7 of the terms.
