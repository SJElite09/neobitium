What Neobitium is

Newer macOS for Macs that cost a lot and still work well.

Neobitium is a one-person service in Newark, Nottinghamshire, run by Ben Mulliss.

  • I install a newer macOS on older Macs, using OpenCore Legacy Patcher, an independent open-source project.
  • You pick the services, see the price straight away and send a booking request.
  • I confirm everything by email.

Which Macs?

Does yours look like one of these?

  • iMac

    All-in-one desktops. Like the 27-inch, Late 2012 in my portfolio.

  • MacBook Pro

    Laptops with a thicker body. Like the 13-inch, Mid 2009.

  • MacBook Air

    Thin, light laptops. Like the 11-inch, Early 2015.

  • Mac mini

    Small desktop boxes that you plug a screen into.

These are simple drawings, not photos of your Mac. Not sure if yours qualifies? Open the Apple menu, choose About This Mac, and email me the model and year.

How it works

From booking to handover.

  1. Choose

    Pick your services in the package builder and see the price.

  2. Book and pay

    Send your booking request. I confirm by email, and you pay then. If I can't do the job, I refund you in full, not including postage costs.

  3. Send your Mac

    I send you my address when you book. You post or bring your Mac.

  4. Upgrade and return

    I upgrade and test it, then send or hand it back with a short summary.
