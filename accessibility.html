Accessibility statement

How this website works for everyone, and what to do if it doesn't.

Last updated 27 October 2026

My aim

I want everyone to be able to use this website. I'm aiming to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

What I've done

  • You can use every page, link, button and form with a keyboard, and there is a "skip to main content" link.
  • Focus is always visible.
  • Headings, lists and landmarks are used to give each page a clear structure.
  • Every form field has a label. Errors are shown in text next to the field, and in a summary at the top of the form.
  • Text colours have been checked against their backgrounds for sufficient contrast, in both light and dark mode.
  • Text resizes, and pages reflow on small screens.
  • Buttons and links say what they do.
  • Every image and diagram has a text description, and decorative icons are hidden from screen readers.
  • Animations are gentle, nothing flashes, nothing plays automatically, and the animations switch off if your device is set to reduce motion.

Known limits

The portfolio screenshots are pictures of screens, so the small text inside them can be hard to read. Each has a text description, and the desktop screenshots open at full size.

The bot check on the booking form is made by Cloudflare, so I can't change how it works. If you can't use it, email me at neobitium@outlook.com and I'll take your booking by email instead. Opening your booking confirmation in a new tab is deliberate, so you can go back to the form if something goes wrong.

How it has been checked

The site has been checked with automated tests for colour contrast and page structure. It hasn't yet been audited by a third party or tested with disabled users, so there may be problems I haven't found.

Tell me about a problem

If you find something hard to use, or you'd like information in a different format, email me at neobitium@outlook.com. Tell me the page and what went wrong, and I'll do my best to fix it.

If you're not happy with my reply, you can contact the Equality Advisory and Support Service: equalityadvisoryservice.com (opens in a new tab).
