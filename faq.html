FAQ

Questions people ask.

Can't see your question? Email me at neobitium@outlook.com.

Will my Mac definitely run a newer macOS?
  • No. It depends on the exact model.
  • I check yours before I start.
  • If it can't take the upgrade, I refund you in full, not including postage costs.
  • Features that need an Apple silicon or T2 security chip, like iPhone Mirroring, won't work on older Macs.
Is it safe for my files?
  • With the back-up add-on, I make a full Time Machine copy of your Mac on my encrypted SSD, and check it's complete.
  • Your files are safe there even if I have to wipe your Mac and start again.
  • That happened once, on the MacBook Air in my portfolio, and the back-up put it right.
  • It's £20 extra, and included in Go Full. I strongly recommend it.
Does Apple endorse this?

No. OpenCore Legacy Patcher is a separate open-source project. Neobitium isn't connected to Apple or to that project.

Which Macs can you upgrade?

Apple-branded Macs where the model suits the upgrade. Email me the model and year (see About This Mac in the Apple menu) and I'll tell you what to expect.

What does Go Full Neobitium include?

Everything, for £120 (worth £145 separately):

  • A full back-up of your Mac, kept on my encrypted SSD for 14 days after I hand it back
  • The Mac software upgrade, with a basic test afterwards
  • A test of which macOS suits your Mac best (several versions installed and tried)
  • A full test of everything afterwards
  • 14 days of aftercare
What's the peace-of-mind pack?
  • A full test of everything after the upgrade.
  • 14 days of aftercare: free fixes for anything I installed or changed that stops working as described.
  • £15 as an add-on, and included in Go Full.
Do you stand behind your work if I don't buy the pack?
  • Yes. Every job comes with your legal rights.
  • If my work isn't done with reasonable care and skill, I put it right for free.
  • The pack adds the thorough test and a clear 14-day window for quick fixes.
  • The terms explain what is and isn't covered.
What does "Which macOS suits it best?" involve?

I install several unsupported macOS versions on the same drive and test each to see which runs best on your Mac. It takes much longer than a single upgrade, so it costs £30 extra.

Do you work on Windows PCs or fix broken Macs?

No. Neobitium is Macs only, so I can do one job properly. I don't do repairs or hardware upgrades.

Do you need my password?
  • I need the password of an administrator account on your Mac.
  • If you'd rather not give me yours, create a new administrator account (in Users & Groups, in System Settings or System Preferences) and give me that password. You can delete the account afterwards.
  • Please don't email passwords. I'll tell you how to pass it on when you book.
How long does a job take?
  • There isn't one number.
  • Some of the time I'm working on the Mac, some of the time I'm waiting beside it, and some of the time it runs by itself.
  • Ask me about your Mac and I'll give you an idea.
Can I cancel?

Yes. You have 14 days to cancel after you book, as long as I haven't started the upgrade. Once I've started, there are no refunds, because I have to finish it. The refunds and cancellation policy has the details.

How do I pay?
  • You pay when you book.
  • I accept cash or bank transfer, and I'll tell you how when I confirm your booking.
  • If I can't do the job, I refund you in full, not including postage costs.
How do I book?
  • Use the package builder: pick your services, fill in the short form and send your booking request.
  • Sending it doesn't charge you.
  • I email you to confirm what's included and the price, and to tell you how to pay.
  • Prefer email? Write to me at neobitium@outlook.com.
Is it worth upgrading my Mac?

Often, but not always. I'd rather tell you not to book than take your money for an upgrade that won't help. The list of reasons it might not be worth it is on the services page.

What do you do with my data?
  • With a back-up, I copy your Mac to my encrypted SSD.
  • I delete the copy 14 days after I hand your Mac back.
  • The website sets no cookies and has no tracking. It only receives what you type into the booking form. See the privacy notice.
How do I get my Mac to you?
  • When you book, I send you my address.
  • You can post your Mac or bring it, and I send or hand it back when it's done.
  • Please don't send anything before you've booked, and pack it carefully.
I'm under 18. Can I book?

Please ask a parent or guardian to get in touch. My services are for people aged 18 or over.
