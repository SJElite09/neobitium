Cookie notice

Short version: this website sets no cookies of its own. The booking page has a Cloudflare bot check.

Last updated 27 October 2026

This website doesn't set cookies of its own

This site doesn't set its own cookies, and it doesn't use local storage or any similar technology of its own to store information on your device or read information from it.

What else it doesn't use

  • No analytics or visitor tracking
  • No advertising or social media pixels
  • No third-party fonts, maps or videos

The fonts you see are already on your device. Everything on the site is served from the same place as the page you're reading, with one exception on the booking page.

The one exception: the bot check on the booking page

The booking form on the package builder page includes Cloudflare Turnstile, a check that tells real people from bots. It loads a script and a small frame from Cloudflare (challenges.cloudflare.com), and it only does this on that page. No other page loads anything from Cloudflare.

  • Cloudflare says it uses the information it gets (your IP address, TLS fingerprint and User-Agent) only to detect and block bots, and to improve the check. See the Cloudflare Turnstile privacy addendum (opens in a new tab).
  • Cloudflare's own documents don't list exactly what, if anything, the check keeps on your device. Cloudflare describes the check as strictly necessary for detecting and blocking bots. It is there to keep spam out of my booking system, and I don't use it for anything else.
  • If you'd rather not load it, you don't have to open the booking page. Email me instead at neobitium@outlook.com.

When you send the booking form

The form sends your details to a Google Apps Script, and the reply opens in a new tab on a Google page (script.google.com). That page belongs to Google, so Google's own cookie and privacy rules apply to it. See the privacy notice for what happens to the details you send.

Why there's no cookie banner

UK rules on cookies and similar technologies (the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations, known as PECR) require consent before storing or accessing information on someone's device, unless an exception applies. This site sets no cookies and stores nothing of its own. The only technology that might store anything on your device is the bot check, which is there to protect the form from abuse and to keep it secure. If that changes, or I find it needs consent, I'll add a banner.

Links to other sites

Some pages link to other websites, such as the ICO, Citizens Advice or Cloudflare. Those sites have their own cookie policies, which I don't control.

Server logs

The server that hosts this site may keep technical logs. These aren't cookies. They are covered in the privacy notice.

If this changes

If I ever add anything that stores or reads information on your device, I'll update this page and ask for your consent first where the law requires it.
