About

Hi, I'm Ben.

I'm a student in Newark, and I help older Macs keep working.

How it started

  • I learned how to install a newer macOS on Macs that Apple has stopped updating.
  • I tried it on Macs I could get hold of, and it worked.
  • Lots of good Macs get written off just because the updates stop, so I thought other people might want the same done for theirs.
  • That's Neobitium.

What I've done so far

  • Three Macs: an iMac, a MacBook Air and a MacBook Pro.
  • Every one is on the portfolio, with before and after screenshots and what doesn't work after the upgrade.
  • It's a new service, and I'd rather say so than pretend to be bigger than I am.

How I work

  • You deal with me directly, from your first email to getting your Mac back.
  • I check your Mac first, and I tell you if I don't think it's worth upgrading.
  • If your Mac can't take the upgrade, you get a full refund, not including postage costs.
  • I recommend a back-up every time.
  • Prices are fixed and on the site.

What I use

  • OpenCore Legacy Patcher, a free, open-source project.
  • I'm not part of that project, and Neobitium isn't connected to it or to Apple.
  • Apple doesn't endorse this method, which is why I'm upfront about the limits.

Got an older Mac?

Tell me the model and year and I'll tell you what to expect.
